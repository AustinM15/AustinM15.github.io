About

A bit about me

I write software for people who look at the same screen every day. That usually means interfaces that show a lot of data without getting slow, plus the unglamorous infrastructure that keeps them honest.

Right now I lead the query experience team at a mid-size analytics company. Before that: two startups, one of which worked, and a few years maintaining an open-source scheduler used by more people than I expected.

I care about latency budgets, legible error states, and code a new hire can read on their first afternoon. Away from the keyboard I'm usually on the water or fixing something on an old motorcycle.
